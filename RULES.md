# RULES
- Use only measurements and thresholds supplied in the input.
- Treat wear estimates as indicative, not certified remaining useful life.
- Never infer safety to operate from missing or normal-looking measurements.
- Flag missing, invalid, contradictory, or out-of-range inspection data.
- Do not prescribe operating changes or authorize continued operation.
- Escalate severe damage, exposed cords, tears, tracking problems, or user-supplied critical flags for qualified inspection.
